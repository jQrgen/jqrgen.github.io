Oslo

Jørgen S. Notland is an open source developer in Oslo. He works on the Nexa blockchain with Bitcoin Unlimited. His public GitHub profile lists that affiliation as “Bitcoin Unlimited / Nexa”. His talks and articles introduce Nexa to developers building products on it.

Nordic Crypto

Bitcoin, blockchain and crypto news from Norway, Sweden, Denmark, Finland and Iceland, in 21 languages, with a conference calendar, talk videos and market prices. Jørgen runs it and publishes the source as the nordic-crypto repository. The newsroom follows the Norwegian press code of ethics, Vær Varsom-plakaten.

Follow it on X and Telegram, or try the TestFlight app for iPhone, iPad and especially Apple TV.
